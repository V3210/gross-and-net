Privacy policy

Gross & Net uploader · Last updated: November 9, 2026

"Gross & Net uploader" is an internal tool used only by the owner of the Gross & Net YouTube channel to publish and manage the channel's own videos. It is not offered to the public and does not collect information from viewers or other users.

What the tool accesses

Using the YouTube API Services, the tool accesses only the Gross & Net channel's own YouTube account, with the owner's permission, in order to:

  • upload videos, thumbnails and caption files, and set titles, descriptions, tags and publishing schedules;
  • manage the channel's playlists and post comments on its own videos;
  • read the channel's own view, watch-time and subscriber statistics.

How information is stored and shared

Access credentials are stored privately by the channel owner and are not shared. The tool does not sell, rent or share any data with third parties, does not use data for advertising, and does not store YouTube data beyond the channel's own video IDs and summary statistics used to improve future videos.

YouTube and Google

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account security settings: myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact the channel through its YouTube page, youtube.com/@grossandnethq.
